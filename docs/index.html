How the score works. Every skill has to point at something real: a language you wrote bytes in, a dependency in a manifest, a file path, or your own words in a README. Roles are weighted baskets of those skills. Openings are scored by how much of your evidence the posting actually speaks to — then reduced for gates you cannot show on GitHub (years of experience, degrees, clearance). Both numbers are shown. Neither is a person.
